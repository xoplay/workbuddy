# Girls Puzzle

! 介绍
经典拼图游戏，通过交换图块复原精美图片。

! 规则
- 滑动或点击交换两个图块
- 将所有图块还原到正确位置即获胜
- 步数有限制，用完则失败

! 玩法
- 点击或拖拽图块进行交换
- 使用道具辅助过关（撤回、提示、一键过关）
- 通关后可收藏图片

! 特色
- 海量精美图片
- 多种难度选择
- 每日挑战模式
- 成就系统

[en]
# Girls Puzzle

! Intro
Classic puzzle game, restore beautiful images by swapping tiles.

! Rules
- Swap two tiles by sliding or tapping
- Win by restoring all tiles to correct positions
- Limited moves, game over when exhausted

! Gameplay
- Tap or drag tiles to swap
- Use tools (undo, hint, fast-win) to assist
- Collect images after clearing levels

! Features
- Massive collection of beautiful images
- Multiple difficulty levels
- Daily challenge mode
- Achievement system
